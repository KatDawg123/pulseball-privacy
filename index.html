PULSEBALL Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how KDGaming (“we,” “us,” or “our”) processes information when you use PULSEBALL, including PULSEBALL on Meta Quest devices.

We aim to collect and process only the information reasonably necessary to operate PULSEBALL, provide multiplayer and social features, maintain player progress, and support the safety and functionality of the game.

1. Information We Process

Meta Horizon account information

When you use PULSEBALL on Meta Horizon, we may receive information made available through Meta platform services, including:

  • Meta Horizon User ID
  • Horizon username or display name
  • Profile information made available through Meta platform services
  • Information about Meta friends who are also available to interact with through PULSEBALL
  • Friend presence or availability information, where provided by Meta
  • Blocked-user relationship information, where provided by Meta
  • Multiplayer invitation information
  • Deep-link or destination information used when joining a PULSEBALL session

We use this information only for features related to player identity, multiplayer, social interaction, invitations, safety, matchmaking, and competitive game features. PULSEBALL does not request your Meta account password or other Meta login credentials.

Multiplayer and session information

PULSEBALL may process PULSEBALL player or squad identifiers, match and lobby identifiers, multiplayer session membership, team assignment, match results, scores, goals and other gameplay statistics, competitive or ranked rating, presence information, and invitation/join-session information.

Game profile and progression information

PULSEBALL may store game-related information such as player identity, level, rank or rating, wins/losses/ties, goals, streaks, cosmetic selections, unlocks, settings, tutorial progress, quests, and other save data. Some information may be stored locally on your device and some may be stored using online game services.

Device and gameplay tracking information

PULSEBALL is a virtual reality game and may process information from your Meta Quest device that is necessary to make the game function, including headset position and orientation, controller position and orientation, hand or controller movement used for gameplay, player movement in the virtual environment, interaction with game objects, and basic device or network information needed to establish and maintain multiplayer connections.

PULSEBALL does not currently use camera imagery for its game features and does not intentionally record microphone audio as part of the current PULSEBALL game experience.

Technical information

Our service providers may process technical information necessary to deliver online services, such as IP address, connection and network information, device or session identifiers, error and diagnostic information, and service request information.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information described above to identify players, display player identity, operate multiplayer matches and lobbies, keep invited friends or squad members together, display available friends, process invitations and deep links, respect blocked-user relationships where supported, operate rankings and leaderboards, save progression and preferences, provide VR movement and interaction, synchronize multiplayer gameplay, prevent abuse, diagnose technical problems, and maintain and improve PULSEBALL.

We do not sell Meta Horizon User Data or Device User Data.

3. Friends, Invites, and Blocked Users

If you use PULSEBALL social features, we may use Meta platform services to identify friends who are available through PULSEBALL and allow multiplayer invitations to be sent. Friend information is used for social and multiplayer features and is not used to create advertising profiles.

Where supported by Meta platform services, PULSEBALL may process blocked-user relationship information to respect user safety preferences and reduce unwanted multiplayer or social interactions.

4. Deep Linking

PULSEBALL may process deep-link information when you accept an invitation or choose to join another player. Deep-link information may identify a PULSEBALL destination, squad, lobby, or multiplayer match so the game can route you to the requested experience.

5. Leaderboards and Competitive Features

PULSEBALL may process player identity and gameplay statistics for competitive ranking and leaderboard features. Leaderboard information may include a player display name or game identity, ranked score, rating, match statistics, or ranking position. We may use Meta platform leaderboard services and/or other game service providers for these features.

6. Service Providers

Meta Platforms Technologies

Meta provides the Meta Horizon platform and platform services used by PULSEBALL, including identity, friends, invitations, deep linking, entitlement, and other supported social or platform functionality.

Photon / Exit Games

PULSEBALL uses Photon services for online multiplayer functionality, including matchmaking, multiplayer rooms, session routing, and networking. Photon may process technical and session information necessary to provide these services.

Unity Technologies

PULSEBALL may use Unity services for game functionality including authentication, player data, cloud services, leaderboards, diagnostics, or related backend functionality. Unity may process information necessary to provide those services.

7. Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers only to the extent reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or support PULSEBALL. We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request. We do not sell personal information obtained through Meta Horizon.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, to operate PULSEBALL, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or maintain the security and integrity of our services. Some information stored locally on your device may remain until you clear the application's data or uninstall PULSEBALL. If information is no longer necessary for PULSEBALL, we will delete or de-identify it as appropriate.
